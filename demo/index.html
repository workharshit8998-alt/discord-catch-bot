Example Verification Portal

This fictional page demonstrates how a convincing login prompt can trick people into sharing secrets.

Do not use your real email, password, login code, or token.

That's the lesson

This demo did not read, transmit, or save anything you typed. A real malicious page may send submitted data to an attacker.

Warning signs

  • Unexpected verification requests or urgent language.
  • A domain that is not an official Discord domain.
  • A page asking for passwords, login codes, or session tokens.
  • Unsolicited DMs and suspicious bot messages.

Stay safe

  • Open Discord directly through its official app or website.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, backup codes, or tokens.
  • If you entered real details, change your password through the official service and enable MFA.
